About this mockup. The pages you clicked through are a picture of what TW Ops will show, built from the TW Ops Hub map. The colours, fonts, wording, hub names and layouts are placeholders and will be changed with Savannah and the office leads. The sample numbers and names are invented. What this page explains is the part that is not a placeholder: how the real system will work under those screens, and why that matters for every app TW builds after this one.

One foundation, every app on top

Today each tool is its own building: BambooHR, Basecamp, Slack, the clinical tools on Render, the huddle library on Netlify. The plan is one foundation that holds everything every app needs and nobody wants to build twice. TW Ops is the first app on it. Dr. Turner's clinical tools are the second. The treatment-coordinator coaching, chair-time and call-review ideas are the third, fourth and fifth.

What is kept out, on purpose

Green · operationalCounts, checklists, tasks, schedules, calendar, inventory, budgets. Lives on the foundation. May go to Slack and email. May be summarized by the AI desk.
Yellow · confidential staff dataEmployee files, I-9s, certifications, reviews, incident reports, contracts, P&L. On the foundation, encrypted, role-restricted, logged. Never in Slack. Pay stays in ADP.
Red · patient informationAnything identifying a patient. Not in TW Ops at all. Plan A: allowed on the foundation for the clinical tools, under signed agreements. Plan B: stays in Cloud 9 and the clinical tools until the upgrade. Never in Slack, never in a claude.ai chat.

Plan A or Plan B

Same foundation, same code, same encryption. The difference is vendor tiers and signed agreements. Cory recommends Plan B. Full detail, costs, sources and the questions your own Claude is likely to raise are on the Foundation Plans page.

Plan A · clinic-grade from day one
≈ $1,450 / month

Every vendor under a signed Business Associate Agreement. Patient data allowed. Clinical tools move in month 1. Point-in-time restore.

Plan B · same house, lighter build · Cory's recommendation
≈ $45–80 / month

Same foundation, same encryption, same code. No patient data: imports write counts only, no patient table. Chosen for the upgrade path: the agreements and settings that make the infrastructure HIPAA-eligible are added when the first patient-data app needs them, with the foundation unchanged. An Option C (Google Sheets, free tiers) is cheaper still but has no path to Plan A.

Why this matters for app #2, #3 and beyond

  • Login built once. Dr. Turner's next app uses the same Google front door on day one.
  • Roles built once. "Office leads see their own office" is a database rule every app inherits.
  • Data crosses apps. TC conversion from the clinical tools reaches the Scorecard without an export.
  • Security once. Encryption, backups, audit log and (in Plan A) agreements cover the foundation; a new app inherits them.
  • Claude builds the next app the right way. One GitHub repository with written house rules. Claude Code reads them and follows the existing patterns.
  • You own it. Every account is in TW's name. Cory is a removable collaborator.
