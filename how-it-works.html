The ten services · plain name first, technical name underneath

DatabasePostgres with row rules

One database for every app. "Who sees what" is enforced here, so no app can skip it.

File vaultDrive and private storage

Documents, certificates and signed forms. Encrypted, and opened only by the right roles.

Night shiftScheduled jobs

Brings in counts from Cloud 9, OrthoAI, ADP and Dentira overnight. Escalates overdue items.

MailroomGmail and alerts

Sends the 6:30 AM briefs by Gmail, and nudges and alerts as they come due.

AI deskClaude, called by the server

Writes the plain sentences in the briefs. Never from a chat window.

Key safeSecrets storage

Passwords and access keys live here, never in the code. Changed on a schedule.

Black boxAudit log and backups

Records who changed what, and when. Automatic backups, with a restore tested monthly.

Test copyA second copy with sample data

Changes are tried here before staff ever see them.

WatchdogMonitoring

Checks the site, the jobs and the briefs. Alerts Cory and Savannah if anything fails.

BlueprintsGitHub with house rules

All the code, owned by TW, with written rules that Claude Code follows for the next app.

What is kept out, on purpose

GreenOperational

Counts, checklists, tasks, schedules, the calendar, inventory and budgets. Lives on the foundation. May go to email and nudges. May be summarized by the AI desk.

YellowConfidential staff data

Employee files, I-9s, certifications, reviews, incident reports, contracts and P&L. On the foundation, encrypted, limited by role and logged. Never in Slack. Pay stays in ADP.

RedPatient information

Anything that identifies a patient. Not in TW Ops at all. Under your Plan B it may sit on the foundation for the clinical tools if you choose: encrypted and protected, but not HIPAA-covered until the Plan A agreements are signed. Never in Slack, never in a claude.ai chat.
